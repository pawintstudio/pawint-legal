Delete your account and data

MineTactics (com.pawintstudio.mines) · Pawint Studio
Last updated: 25 September 2026

This page explains how to have your MineTactics account and the data attached to it deleted. It applies to the account the Game creates for you when you first sign in with Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iOS).

Delete it yourself, in the Game

The fastest way is inside the Game, and it takes effect immediately — you do not need to write to us or wait.

  1. Open the Game and go to Settings (the gear icon in the lobby).
  2. Tap Delete account at the bottom of the sheet, then tap it once more to confirm.

Your account and the cloud save attached to it are deleted right then. If you have already uninstalled the Game, install it again and follow the same two steps — signing in with the same Google Play Games or Game Center account brings you back to the account you want removed.

Or ask us to delete it

If you would rather not install the Game again, write to us instead.

  1. Open the Game and go to Settings. Your player ID is shown there.
  2. Email pawintstudio@gmail.com from any address, with the subject “delete my data” and your player ID in the message.

We need the player ID because the Game holds no name, email address, or phone number for you — it is the only way we can find your data. We will confirm by reply and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Deletion is permanent. Your game progress, purchased items, and in-game currencies cannot be recovered afterwards, and purchases are not refunded. If you install the Game again, you start over.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Player ID and the account record tied to it Deleted
Cloud save — stage results, stars, in-game currencies, cards, account level, settings Deleted
Gameplay events already sent to Unity Analytics Unity Analytics deletion is requested, so the events can no longer be linked to you
Google Analytics for Firebase app-usage events Data stored on this device is cleared and the app-instance ID is reset. Events already received by Google follow its retention settings; the in-game account deletion does not erase those server records.
Crash diagnostics Retained by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days, then deleted automatically
Purchase records Kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires, because we are obliged to keep them. They are not used for anything else.

Deleting the Game only

Uninstalling the Game removes the save stored on your device, but does not delete the cloud save. Use the request above if you want the cloud copy gone as well.

Your platform account

Your Google Play Games and Game Center accounts belong to Google and Apple, not to us. Deleting your MineTactics data does not touch them, and we cannot delete them for you.
