Privacy Policy

Realm Rush: State Conquest (com.pawintstudio.realmrush.android) · Pawint Studio
Effective date: October 19, 2026

Pawint Studio ("we", "us") respects the privacy of players of the mobile game Realm Rush (the "Game"). This policy explains what information the Game processes, why, who it is shared with, and what choices you have. It applies to the Game only. Pawint Studio is the developer of the Game and the controller of this information.

At a glance. The Game does not request your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, photos, contacts or precise location through gameplay or sign-in. If you contact support by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. On Android you sign in with Google Play Games. The Game uses a game player ID to save your progress, and the advertising SDKs use your device's advertising ID and device information to show ads. The game player ID is also given to the ad mediation SDK so that ad rewards can be matched to your game account.

1. Information we process

The Game signs you in with the Google Play Games account already on your device, so there is no separate account to create and no password to give us. Google Play Games returns a player identifier and a one-time authorization code, which the Game passes to Unity Authentication. Unity Authentication issues the game player ID that your save is stored under. We never receive your Google password, and we do not receive your email address.

Notifications ("your daily reward is waiting") are scheduled on your device only. The Game does not use a push-notification server and does not collect a push token. You can turn them off in the Game under Settings → Push Alarm or in Android's settings.

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message, and use them only to answer you.

2. Why we use it

  • To run the Game — sign you in, save and restore your progress, currencies and items, and apply daily resets.
  • To process in-app purchases — verify purchases, deliver what you bought to the game account that bought it without delivering it twice, keep Remove Ads active, and handle refunds, disputes and support requests.
  • To show ads — serve rewarded and full-screen ads, attribute ad rewards to your game account, measure ad performance, and personalize ads where permitted.
  • Fraud prevention and security — detect ad fraud, purchase abuse and tampering with the Game.
  • To improve the Game — find and fix crashes, and balance difficulty and rewards using gameplay statistics.
  • To answer your emails and handle requests about your data.

Legal bases (EEA and UK users)

  • Performance of a contract — signing you in, saving and restoring your progress, and delivering purchases.
  • Legitimate interests — crash diagnostics, fraud and tamper prevention, and showing non-personalized ads.
  • Consent — gameplay statistics and personalized advertising (section 3).
  • Legal obligation — keeping purchase records where the law requires it.

3. Your choices: statistics and advertising

Gameplay statistics

If your device appears to be in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland (judged from the device's region setting and time zone), the Game does not collect gameplay statistics unless you allow it. If you decline, or do not answer, none are collected and the Game works exactly as it does otherwise. You can change your answer at any time in the Game under Settings → Usage stats; switching it off stops collection right away. Deleting your account (section 7) also asks Unity Analytics to delete the gameplay statistics already collected.

Advertising

The Game shows two kinds of ads: rewarded ads, which you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward, and full-screen ads that may appear when you enter a stage. The Game does not show banner ads and does not use web cookies.

  • Advertising ID. You can delete or reset your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads. You will still see ads, but they will not be personalized.
  • EEA, the UK and Switzerland. The Game asks for your choice about personalized ads before any ad is requested. If you decline, or do not answer, the ads you see are not personalized. You can change your answer at any time in the Game under Settings → Personalized ads.
  • Remove Ads. The Game offers a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase. After it, full-screen ads are no longer shown, rewards that normally require watching an ad are granted without one, and on later launches the Game does not start the advertising SDKs.

We do not build advertising profiles ourselves.

7. Your rights and how to delete your data

You may ask to access, correct or delete your information, or to object to or restrict its processing, at any time. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and the UK, the CCPA/CPRA in California, or the Personal Information Protection Act in Korea), you may have additional rights, including the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. There are two ways to delete your data; the details are on the Delete your account and data page.

Delete it yourself, in the Game

  1. Open Settings in the Game and tap Delete account at the bottom.
  2. Tap Delete to confirm. The Game deletes your cloud save and the purchase history stored with it, then the game account itself, and then the game data and the privacy choices stored on your device.

You need an internet connection; if you are offline the deletion does not start and nothing is changed. Once it has started it cannot be cancelled, and if it is interrupted the Game finishes it the next time it starts. Afterwards the Game asks for your agreement again, and if you sign in you start over with a new game account. Deleting the game account does not delete your Google account. What is removed and what stays is listed on the deletion page.

Or ask us by email

Email pawintstudio@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and the details listed on the deletion page. Because we hold no name or email address for you, we cannot find your data from those; we will tell you if we need more to identify the account. We complete the deletion within 30 days and reply to confirm. Purchase records that we must keep by law are stored separately and deleted when that period ends.

Deleted progress, currencies and items cannot be restored. You can delete or reset your advertising ID in Android settings to manage its use for future ads. This does not itself delete information already held by advertising providers; use the privacy controls in their linked policies to request deletion.

8. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 14 in Korea, or the applicable age in your country). If we learn that we have, we will delete it promptly. Parents or guardians can contact us at pawintstudio@gmail.com, and may use the methods in section 7 on a child's behalf.

9. Security

Data sent between the Game and servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Server data is stored in Unity Gaming Services with access controls, and we keep internal access to a minimum. We do not store your real name or contact details together with your game data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.
