Delete your account and data

Realm Rush: State Conquest (com.pawintstudio.realmrush.android) · Pawint Studio
Last updated: October 19, 2026

Realm Rush uses Google Play Games sign-in on Android and saves your progress to the game account associated with it. This page explains how to delete that game account and the data attached to it. Deleting the game account does not delete your Google account.

Delete it yourself, in the Game

This starts deletion directly in the Game. You do not need to send an email for the game account and ordinary cloud save; an internet connection is required to complete the process.

  1. Open the Game and open Settings from the lobby.
  2. Tap Delete account at the bottom of the Settings window.
  3. A confirmation window warns that all progress and purchases will be erased for good. Tap Delete to confirm, or Cancel to go back.

You need an internet connection. If you are offline, the deletion does not start and nothing is changed — try again once you are connected. The Game deletes your cloud save first, then the game account, and only then the data on your device. Once it has started it cannot be cancelled: if the connection drops part-way, the Game finishes the deletion the next time it starts, or when you tap Delete account again, before that account can be used again. When it is done, the Game returns to the title screen and asks for your agreement to the terms again; if you play again, you start over with a new game account.

If you have already uninstalled the Game, install it again and sign in with the same Google Play Games account — that brings you back to the account you want removed — then follow the steps above.

Or ask us by email

If you cannot or would rather not install the Game again, write to us instead.

  1. Email pawintstudio@gmail.com with the subject "Realm Rush data deletion request". You can request deletion without reinstalling or opening the Game.
  2. Include what you can of the following, so that we can find the account: the Player ID shown in the Game's Settings, a Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.) if you ever bought something in the Game, your device model, and roughly when you installed the Game. If you still have access to the Game, the contact/support option in Settings prepares an email containing your full game player ID. You can use that email to make the request.
  3. We will verify the account and your authority to request deletion, complete a verified request within 30 days, and reply to confirm. Do not send your Google password or verification codes.

The Game holds no name, email address or phone number for you, so we cannot find your data from those. If the details you send are not enough to identify the account, we will tell you and ask for more.

Deletion is permanent. Your progress, in-game currencies and items cannot be recovered afterwards, and purchased benefits — including Remove Ads — are not restored. Deletion does not automatically issue a refund. Your statutory refund and cancellation rights are unaffected.

What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Game player ID and the account record (Unity Gaming Services) Deleted
Cloud save — stage results and stars, account level, in-game currencies, commanders, artifacts, runes and other items, quest, pass and attendance state, Remove Ads status, and the purchase history stored with your save Deleted. The Game deletes this data itself, before it deletes the account record.
Records written by our support team — only if you ever contacted us and we adjusted your account (for example a compensation grant or a save repair) These records are separate from the ordinary cloud save and are not removed by the in-game deletion process. Include them in your email deletion request; we remove them within 30 days after verifying the request, except records we must retain by law.
Game data stored on this device Deleted with in-game deletion: the local save and its backups, locally stored progress, the Player ID shown in Settings, and your privacy choices (your agreement to the terms and your answers about usage statistics and personalized ads) — the Game asks for them again the next time it starts.
Kept on purpose: device settings (sound, language, vibration and notification switches), a marker of the sign-in method used on this device, and a small purchase-safety record (identifiers of recent Google Play purchases, each with a hashed account tag). The purchase-safety record stops a purchase made by the deleted account from being delivered to a different account on this device. Android backup or device-transfer copies may need to be removed separately through your device or Google account settings.
Gameplay statistics already sent to Unity Analytics With in-game deletion, the Game sends Unity Analytics a deletion request. Processing is handled by Unity and may not finish at the same time as deletion of your game account.
Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) Firebase keeps reports and associated identifiers for 90 days before starting removal from live and backup systems. With in-game deletion, crash reporting on this device is switched off until you agree to the terms again.
Data collected by advertising providers, including the game player ID given to the ad mediation SDK Handled under each provider's policy linked in our Privacy Policy. Deleting the game account or resetting the advertising ID does not itself erase data already held by those providers. Use their privacy controls to request deletion. Android Settings → Google → Ads lets you manage the advertising ID used for future ads.
Purchase records The purchase history in the cloud save is deleted with that save. Google Play independently retains order records under Google's policies and applicable law. Transaction records we are legally required to retain (for example, payment records for 5 years in Korea) are kept separately for the required period and then deleted, and are not used for unrelated purposes.

Deleting the Game only

Uninstalling the Game normally removes its local app data, but does not delete the game account or the cloud save. Android backups may be retained or restored separately. Use one of the two ways above to delete the game account and its cloud data.

Your Google account

Your Google and Google Play Games accounts belong to Google, not to us. Deleting your Realm Rush data does not touch them, and we cannot delete them for you.
