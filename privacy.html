Privacy Policy

MineTactics (com.pawintstudio.mines) · Pawint Studio
Effective date: 16 August 2026 · Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game MineTactics ("the Game") collects, why it is collected, and who it is shared with. It applies to the Game only. Pawint Studio ("we", "us") is the developer and the controller of this information.

The Game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, photos, contacts, or precise location, and we do not collect them.

To save your progress, the Game signs you in with the gaming account of your platform — Google Play Games on Android, Game Center on iOS. It uses the account already present on your device, so there is nothing to create and no password to enter. We receive only the player identifier that service returns to us; we never receive your Google or Apple password, and we do not receive your email address.

2. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

  • Performance of a contract — saving and restoring your progress, and delivering purchases.
  • Legitimate interests — crash diagnostics, and fraud and cheat prevention.
  • Consent — gameplay analytics and personalised advertising.
    • If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before it collects any gameplay analytics. If you decline, or do not answer, none are collected and the Game works exactly as it does otherwise. You can change your answer at any time in the Game under Settings, and a deletion request (section 6) also requests deletion of Unity Analytics gameplay events already collected. The Game resets the Firebase Analytics identifier and on-device data; events already received by Google follow its retention settings.
    • If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, the Game shows a consent form provided by Google's User Messaging Platform (a Google-certified consent management platform) before any ad is requested. It lists our ad partners (Google, Unity Ads and ironSource) and lets you consent, decline, or choose per purpose. If you decline, or do not answer, the ads you see are not personalised. You can change your answer at any time in the Game under Settings → Personalized ads, which reopens the form.

3. Advertising choices

Rewarded ads are optional — you choose whether to watch one. You can limit ad personalisation at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalisation.
  • In the Game: Settings → Personalized ads turns ad personalisation on or off. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland it opens the consent form described in section 2, and ads are not personalised until you consent there.
  • The Game also offers a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase. It removes the full-screen ads shown after a cleared stage. Rewarded ads that you choose to watch still play and use the ad network as described above.

4. Who your information is shared with

We share information only with the service providers listed below, and only for the purposes above. Each of them processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • Unity Technologies — Authentication, Cloud Save, Cloud Code, Analytics, Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads advertising: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Google LLC — Play Games Services sign-in (Android), Firebase Crashlytics, Google Analytics for Firebase, Google Play services and Billing, Google AdMob advertising and the User Messaging Platform consent form: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple Inc. — Game Center sign-in (iOS): apple.com/legal/privacy

These providers operate servers outside your country, including in the United States. Transfers rely on the safeguards described in the providers' own policies, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. How long it is kept

  • Game progress is kept while your player ID exists, so that you do not lose your save. It is deleted when you ask us to delete it (see below).
  • Crash reports are retained by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Analytics and advertising data are retained according to the retention settings of the providers listed above, and are not linked by us to any real-world identity.

6. Your rights and how to delete your data

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the information tied to your player ID, or to object to or restrict its processing. Because we hold no name or email address for you, we cannot find your data from those — to make a request, contact us from the device where you play and include your player ID, shown in the Game under Settings.

Deleting the Game's data: uninstalling the Game removes the local save from your device but does not delete the cloud save. To delete the cloud save, email us at pawintstudio@gmail.com with your player ID and the words "delete my data". We will delete it within 30 days. Deletion is permanent and your game progress cannot be recovered afterwards. Purchase records are held by the store and are retained where we are required to keep them for tax and accounting purposes.

If you are in the EEA or the UK you may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. If you are in the Republic of Korea, you may contact the Personal Information Protection Commission.

7. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data in transit is protected with TLS, and cloud saves are stored by Unity Gaming Services under its own security controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game changes. The "Last updated" date at the top will change, and material changes will be announced in the Game before they take effect. Continuing to play after a change means you accept the updated policy.
